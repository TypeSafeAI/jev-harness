# Jev Harness screenshot and evidence protocol

For the optional interface, use its documented offline/demo state with synthetic fixtures. For the headless contract, a recorded offline benchmark transcript is more useful than an invented product screen. The editorial SVG is not a screenshot.

Useful evidence shows the proposed synthetic action, validation result, review evidence, and the final outcome together. Keep the evidence-not-authorization distinction visible. Do not relabel a scripted benchmark as a live Jev measurement or replace an unavailable result with a favorable one.

Do not start a live Codex arena, call a provider, apply a patch, or approve a tool solely to capture marketing media. Live experiments require a separate explicit scope and provenance record. Use fake transports and fixture hosts for automated tests.

Record source commit, fixture IDs, question-set version, model label where relevant, command, viewport, theme, and local/preview/production environment. Include skipped checks and unavailable data rather than filling them with invented values. Follow the [shared screenshot protocol](https://github.com/TypeSafeAI/.github/blob/main/docs/discovery/SCREENSHOTS.md).

This documentation change does not claim a fresh screenshot, live experiment, or deployment.
